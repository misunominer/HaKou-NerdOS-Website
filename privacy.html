Privacy Policy

Ha•Kou NerdOS — Last updated: September 8, 2026

Ha•Kou NerdOS respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit the Ha•Kou NerdOS website, contact the project, or use optional Ha•Kou NerdOS features.

1. Website Information

The public Ha•Kou NerdOS website is designed to collect as little personal information as reasonably possible. The website may store basic preferences, such as your selected language, locally in your browser.

2. Contact by Email

If you contact Ha•Kou NerdOS by email, the information you voluntarily provide — such as your email address, name, message and any attachments — may be retained as necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain appropriate communication records.

The official public contact address is contact@hakounerdos.com.

3. Technical and Infrastructure Data

Infrastructure and security services used to operate the website may process limited technical information such as IP addresses, request information, security events and similar network data when necessary to deliver, protect and maintain the service.

4. Mining and Missions

Mining and Missions are independent components of Ha•Kou NerdOS. Participation in Missions is optional and is not required for Mining to operate.

Current Mission points are test points only. They have no monetary value, cannot be withdrawn, and do not represent a promise of payment or future financial reward.

5. Future Third-Party Services

Ha•Kou NerdOS may integrate optional third-party services in the future, including services related to Missions or rewards. If such integrations are introduced, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain what information is processed, why it is processed, and which third parties are involved before those services are made available to users.

6. Data Sharing

Ha•Kou NerdOS does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when technically necessary to operate, secure or deliver the website and related services, or when disclosure is required by applicable law.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for security and operational needs, or to meet applicable legal obligations.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect Ha•Kou NerdOS services. However, no internet-connected system or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Ha•Kou NerdOS develops and new features or services are introduced. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to contact@hakounerdos.com.
